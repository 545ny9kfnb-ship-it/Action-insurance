What to check before requesting help

Requirements depend on the notice and your circumstances. Use the exact information from your official notice when contacting the office.

Read the official notice

Check the filing type, deadline, reference number and instructions from the Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (FLHSMV). Contact FLHSMV if it is unclear what your notice requires.

Ask about the required filing

Tell Action Insurance you are asking about an SR-22 and have your notice available. The insurer must confirm whether it can offer a policy and make the filing required for your case.

Confirm submission and status

Ask who will submit the filing, when it is expected and how to verify that FLHSMV has recorded it. Do not assume a quote request or policy purchase alone satisfies a notice.

Ask before changing coverage

Check with the insurer or FLHSMV about ongoing requirements before changing or cancelling coverage. The requirements depend on your notice and official records.

Florida's official guidance

FLHSMV describes an SR-22 as a filing certifying liability insurance for certain reinstatement requirements. Your specific notice controls what applies. Review Florida's insurance requirements from FLHSMV and contact the agency about your case if anything is unclear. Action Insurance can discuss its listed SR-22 assistance but cannot replace official instructions or guarantee that a filing satisfies them.

SR-22 questions

Is an SR-22 itself an insurance policy?

No. It is a filing or certificate associated with insurance. Ask the insurer what policy and filing are required for your particular notice.

Can I ask for help before I know all the details?

Yes. Contact the office to ask what information is needed. Having the notice and relevant vehicle and driver details may help clarify your request.

Can a filing date be guaranteed?

A quote request does not guarantee a filing date or outcome. Ask the insurer about timing and verify filing status with FLHSMV.
