What to share about a work vehicle

A vehicle used for business may need a different quote discussion from a personal car. Tell the office how the vehicle is used and ask what options are available.

Vehicle and driver details

Have the ZIP code, vehicle details and VIN, number of vehicles, and information about the drivers ready. The office or insurer can tell you what additional details are needed for your request.

Business use

Describe the business, the work the vehicle supports and how it is used. Clear details help distinguish a work-vehicle question from a personal auto quote request.

Policy questions

Ask which vehicles and drivers would be listed, what activities the policy applies to and whether any requested change has taken effect. The insurer confirms the policy terms.

Availability and price

Commercial auto quote availability, eligibility, price and terms depend on the insurer and application. A quote request does not guarantee that a policy will be offered.

Not sure what to request?
