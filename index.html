A FEW QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

Before we talk.

Is the quote request really free?

Yes. You can request a free, no-obligation quote. The insurer determines available coverage, eligibility, and pricing. Requesting a quote does not mean coverage is in effect.

Do you help customers outside Belle Glade?

Yes. Our office is in Belle Glade, and we assist customers throughout Florida. Let us know your ZIP code and the type of insurance you’re looking for.

Can you help with trucks and work vehicles?

Yes. Truck and commercial auto insurance are among the services we offer. Tell us about your business, vehicles, and drivers so we can discuss your needs and available options.

Will all 18 companies offer a quote for my situation?

Available companies vary by the coverage you need, your location, and your application. Our agency represents 18 companies, but every company may not offer every product or accept every applicant.

What if I already have insurance?

You can ask us to review your coverage needs and explore available options. Keep your existing coverage in place until any replacement policy and its effective date are confirmed.
